## 1. The finding (VCC only)

On VCC validation, `LinearRegression` fit on VCC train:

| model | features | val R² | val Spearman |
|---|---|---|---|
| COUNT-LINEAR | `log1p(n_cells)` | **+0.4325** | +0.8184 |
| MAG-LINEAR | `m̃` (four scalars) | +0.2589 | +0.6660 |
| MAG+COUNT | `[m̃ ; log1p(n_cells)]` | +0.4342 | +0.8079 |

**Magnitude adds +0.0017 beyond cell count.** Cell count alone out-predicts the magnitude
block by 0.17 R².

The mechanism is not in dispute: VCC's endpoint is **not size-controlled**
(`src/data/vcc/loader.py:394` — `anderson_ksamp` over all of a construct's cells, no
subsample), unlike the harmonized **external** endpoint (§6.3, 27/788 fixed subsamples).
So `ρ(n_cells, y) = +0.70` while `ρ(n_cells, m̃) = −0.72`, and the magnitude↔label
relationship on VCC runs largely through `n`.

Partial rank correlation with `y`, controlling `log1p(n_cells)`:
`mean|x| −0.095`, `‖x‖₂ −0.010`, **`max|x| +0.379`**, `σ −0.010`.
Only `max|x|` carries a substantial count-independent association — and it *strengthens*
under adjustment (pooled +0.107 → partial +0.379), the signature of a suppressed
association.

## 3. Two secondaries, pre-registered and fully specified

Both are **secondary sensitivity analyses**. Neither substitutes for the primary under any
result. Neither may be promoted after the numbers land.

### 3.1 MAX-LINEAR

`sklearn.linear_model.LinearRegression(fit_intercept=True)`, defaults otherwise, on the
**single** feature `max|x|` — the same `‖x‖∞` component of the existing `m̃` block,
z-scored with the frozen VCC-train scaler (§6B). Fit **once** on VCC train, applied
**zero-shot** to RPE1 and K562-essential with no target-side calibration. No tuning, no
selection, no refit.

It introduces **no new information**: `max|x|` is already one of the four `m̃` scalars.
Being one-dimensional, it cannot fail the way the App. F RF configuration did on the 4-D
block.

### 3.2 MAG+COUNT

Same estimator and discipline, on `[m̃ ; log1p(n_cells)]` — the four z-scored magnitude
scalars plus `log1p(n_cells)` **z-scored on VCC-train statistics**, so the count feature
is on the same footing as the rest of the block.

**Cell counts must come from the same cell set as that screen's `x_cell` / `m̃` block**, so
the features are internally consistent. RPE1 has two cell sets (L13: embedding-compatible
min 27 / checkpoint min 25) — **record which was used**; do not mix.

### 3.3 A limitation of MAG+COUNT, recorded in advance

VCC's cell counts run 10–121. RPE1's run 25–3,451 and K562's 27–1,996. The count feature
is therefore **far outside its training range on both external screens**. A linear head
extrapolates rather than saturating, so this does not fail the way a tree would — but the
external MAG+COUNT number is partly an extrapolation of a coefficient fitted on a narrow
range. **State this alongside the number.** It is recorded now so it is not discovered as
a convenient explanation afterwards.

## 4. Interpretation, fixed before the numbers exist

The structure generates a sharp prediction, and it is written down here so it can be wrong:

**If** VCC's magnitude↔label relationship is largely count-mediated, **and** the external
endpoint is size-controlled (§6.3), **then** what should transfer is the
count-*independent* component. Concretely:

- `MAX-LINEAR` transferring comparably to or better than the primary magnitude arm →
  consistent with the count-independent component carrying the transfer.
- `MAG+COUNT` transferring **worse** than `MAX-LINEAR` → consistent with the count-mediated
  part not surviving a size-controlled endpoint.
- Both transferring poorly → the magnitude transfer claim is weak on the externals
  regardless of decomposition, and that is the finding.
- `MAG+COUNT` transferring **best** → the prediction above is **refuted**; record the
  refutation as plainly as a confirmation.

None of these outcomes changes the primary. They inform how it is described.

## 5. Four manuscript disclosures, non-negotiable

These hold regardless of what Phase 4 returns:

1. **VCC's endpoint is not size-controlled** (`loader.py:394`), while the external
   harmonized endpoint is (§6.3, 27/788). The submitted manuscript does not state this
   asymmetry.
2. **On VCC validation, `log1p(n_cells)` alone reaches R² +0.4325 against the magnitude
   block's +0.2589**, and magnitude adds +0.0017 beyond it.
3. **After controlling for cell count, only `max|x|` retains a substantial association**
   (+0.379); the three averaging components collapse to ≈0.
4. **The magnitude features are not size-matched across screens** (`loader.py:403`,
   `x_cell_accumulator.py:178` — means over all cells; a 10-cell floor is not matching),
   and the three screens' cell-count supports are **disjoint at the interquartile level**
   (VCC [18,40], RPE1 [48,113], K562 [79,174]).

All four are reproducible from the released code. A reviewer who runs it will find them.

## 6. A framing question this amendment does not decide

Disclosure (2) bears on the manuscript's central claim and its title. That is an
an authorial decision about how the paper is framed, not an experimental commitment,
and it is deliberately **not** settled here. What is settled: the experiments do not
change, and the four disclosures are made.

It should be settled before the submission deadline. The finding is
recoverable — a paper reporting that the naive magnitude signal is confounded by sampling
depth, and that a specific component survives adjustment and *strengthens*, is a more
careful paper than one that reports the confounded version. But the reframing has to be
decided by people, not inferred from a table.
