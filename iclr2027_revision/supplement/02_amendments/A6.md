# Lock amendment A6 — editorial wording only

**Committed 2026-08-26, after external unblinding.** Amendments A1 through A4 were
committed before the measurements they govern existed. A5 and this amendment were
committed after external unblinding, and both are editorial: A5 rewords the Findings
paragraph of the Introduction, and A6 is the wording change below. Neither changes a
hypothesis, a control, a seed, an endpoint, a statistic, a reported value or a
conclusion.

## Change

Four occurrences of "genuine signal" / "genuinely" in the manuscript's authoritative
framing blocks are replaced with "measurable predictive information" or the
grammatically appropriate equivalent.

| Location | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Abstract | "the frozen representation carries **genuine signal** beyond a capacity-matched control" | "the frozen representation carries **measurable predictive information** beyond a capacity-matched control" |
| §1, Findings | "the frozen representation carries **genuine in-distribution signal**" | "the frozen representation carries **measurable in-distribution predictive information**" |
| §1, Contributions | "carries **genuine VCC in-distribution signal** beyond a capacity-matched control" | "carries **measurable VCC in-distribution predictive information** beyond a capacity-matched control" |
| §6.6, Synthesis | "the frozen representation is **genuinely informative** in-distribution" | "the frozen representation is **measurably informative** in-distribution" |

## Rationale

"Genuine" asserts a property of the underlying biology. What the study establishes is that
a frozen representation supports above-floor prediction of the label on a held-out split —
a statement about measurable predictive information, not about authenticity or mechanism.
The paper is careful everywhere else; these four words were not, and a reviewer is entitled
to ask what "genuine" does that "measurable" does not.
