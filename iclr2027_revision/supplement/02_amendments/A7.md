  augmented arm remains negative — magnitude still improves but does not rescue transfer.

The non-overlapping subset (≈98% of constructs) reproduces the primary picture; no conclusion is overturned,
softened, or strengthened beyond negligible shifts (|Δρ| ≤ 0.014). Reported as the pre-registered secondary,
not as a replacement for the primary.
