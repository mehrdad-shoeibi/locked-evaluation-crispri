## The open item

Lock §31 M1 (also §37) records:

> `|G_tr|` = 140 (manuscript §3) versus 150 unique target genes in the Training obs. …
> Determine whether the manuscript's 140 reflects a documented eligibility or minimum-cell
> filter the raw count omits — in which case say so — or whether the number is wrong.
> Record the resolution.

The item was marked BLOCKING and had remained open because the earlier evidence-recovery
pass (R5) searched the lock itself, where the question was posed, rather than the
implementation preflight, where the answer already existed. That is a search-scope failure,
not a data problem.

## Resolution: 140 is correct. The manuscript's number stands.

The split counts in manuscript §3 are **post-eligibility** counts. The eligibility rule is
the feature path's existing floor: a target gene is retained only if it has **at least ten
cells in some (target gene, batch) group** (`min_cells = 10`). Applied to the raw split:

| Split | Raw unique targets | Removed by eligibility | Eligible (manuscript) | Rows |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Train | 150 | 10 | **140** | 5,740 |
| Validation | 50 | 1 | **49** | 2,052 |
| Test | 100 | 7 | **93** | 3,972 |
| **Total** | **300** | **18** | **282** | **11,764** |

**Every one of the 18 excluded genes is explained by that rule, with none left over.** The
reconciliation is exact in both directions: no gene is excluded that the rule retains, and
no gene is retained that the rule excludes. The `min_cells = 10` floor is the same
threshold already recorded in amendment A3 §2 as the feature path's existing floor, so this
is not a newly introduced criterion.

Nothing in the manuscript required a numerical change. What was missing was the sentence
saying the counts are post-eligibility.

## Manuscript change

Manuscript §3 (Problem setup, Data paragraph) now reads:

> The set of target genes is partitioned disjointly into G_tr, G_val, and G_te of sizes
> 140, 49, and 93, giving 5,740 training, 2,052 validation, and 3,972 test rows. These are
> counts after eligibility, which requires at least ten cells in some (target gene, batch)
> group and removes 10, 1, and 7 genes from the 150, 50, and 100 raw targets; every
> excluded gene is accounted for by that rule, with none left over.

A reviewer holding the public VCC data can now check the number rather than having to
trust it.

## The consequence, traced and closed

Resolving M1 put three distinct sets in play, and they must not be conflated:

1. **raw training targets** — 150 unique target genes in the training obs;
2. **eligible training targets `G_tr`** — 140, the manuscript's quantity;
3. **the non-overlap exclusion set** — the target genes removed from the external screens
   in the pre-registered non-overlap sensitivity.

The specification and execution records establish that (3) was built
from (1), the raw 150, in both the specification and the execution.

**Specified.** A7 §38-40 defines the exclusion over "external constructs whose
`target_symbol` occurs among the 150 unique VCC-training target genes
(`adata_Training.h5ad` obs, excluding non-targeting)". The same wording appears in
`R6_RESULTS.md`:121-122. Lock §25 fixes only the counts, not the universe.

**Executed.** No persisted execution artifact from the R6 Task B run exists — no exclusion
list, log, or constructing code — so the executed universe was established by
count-fingerprint reconstruction from persisted inputs, and independently corroborated:

| Screen | recorded exclusion | `RAW150 ∩ T` | `ELIG140 ∩ T` | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| RPE1 | 42 genes / 43 constructs | 42 / 43 ✓ | 36 / 37 ✗ | RAW150 |
| K562-essential | 32 genes / 33 constructs | 32 / 33 ✓ | 28 / 29 ✗ | RAW150 |

The discriminator `D = RAW150 \ ELIG140` (the ten min-cells drops: ATP6V0C, BRD9, DNAJA3,
EPHB4, FDPS, OXA1L, RNF20, SALL4, SLC39A6, TAF13) intersects the external targets on both
screens — six genes on RPE1, four on K562-essential — and every one of them is inside the
recorded exclusion, which only the raw universe produces. The integrity check holds: one
gene, HIRA, carries two constructs in each screen, matching 43/42 and 33/32.

Corroboration from a second, independent line: [FEATURE-PIPELINE]'s `excluded_vcc_training_overlap` is
itself raw-based — `phasec_rpe1_ad_label_generation_v1.py`:137 builds `vcc_train_targets`
from the unfiltered training obs — so the alternative hypothesis that the list was
inherited from [FEATURE-PIPELINE] collapses into the same answer rather than competing with it.

**Evidence class, stated plainly.** The verdict rests on reconstruction plus the [FEATURE-PIPELINE]
source, not on a stored Task-B artifact; the 42 and 32 gene names are not persisted
anywhere, only their cardinalities. Two independent lines agree, so this is not a
single-source finding, but the exclusion list itself remains reconstructible rather than
archived, and the code release should say so.

**Consequence for the paper.** The choice is not immaterial: under the eligible 140 the
sensitivity would have removed 37 constructs on RPE1 and 29 on K562-essential instead of
43 and 33. The raw set is the more conservative of the two rules, simply because it
contains the eligible set and so removes strictly more. The extra six constructs on RPE1
and four on K562-essential carry no exposure risk in any case: a gene the cell-count floor
removed from training contributes no training row, so the model never saw it. Occurring
among the raw 150 is not the same as having been seen during training, and neither the
manuscript nor this amendment should be read as claiming otherwise. The manuscript states
the basis in Sec. 6.2 and gives the full comparison in App. D.

**A7 is not amended.** Specified and executed match, so there is nothing in A7 to correct;
and a filed pre-registration amendment is not the place for a retrospective clarification
in any case. This amendment carries it instead.

## Closure of §31 M6 (encoder width)

Lock §31 M6 records an internal inconsistency in the manuscript: the SPSM V4 encoder
stated as 128-dimensional at `main.tex`:462 and :1007 while :1004-1005 already said 256.

**M6 was correct when it was filed, and the defect has since been fixed.** The 128 existed
in the Overleaf draft of 2026-08-21 at exactly the lines M6 cites. It was corrected during
the revision. The current manuscript states 256 in all four places where the encoder
dimension appears (§5.1, App. L twice, App. N), and the only surviving 128 is the
MAG-Anchor residual branch `d_in → 256 → 128 → 1`, which is a different network.

The code was never 128 for this encoder: `hidden_dim = 256` in `src/models/encoders.py`,
`families.py`, `configs/models/[LEGACY-MODEL-CONFIG].yaml` and `configs/base.yaml`. The `hidden_dim = 128`
sites in the codebase belong to the MAG-Anchor branch and to a two-layer exploratory
variant that produced no reported number.

**§31 M6 is therefore marked RESOLVED.** It is recorded here rather than left live because
a supplement that ships an unresolved inconsistency the manuscript no longer contains
invites exactly the question this amendment chain exists to pre-empt.
