## 0. What A15 is, and what it is not

A15 records wording inconsistencies discovered after the A14/v3 release audit and authorizes
their repair. It is a wording instrument only.

**A15 is not** a scientific amendment, a new hypothesis, a new pre-registration, a new evaluation
decision, a new held-out access authorization, a model change, a metric change, or a result change.

**A15 changes no** dataset, endpoint, split, control, seed, feature representation, model, head,
metric, estimator, primary result, secondary result, or numerical value. It performs no
computation of any kind.

## 1. The governing evidence standard

The surviving evidence standard, established by the A13/A14 chain and unchanged here, is:

> **The local record positively locates the first documented external-outcome access at final
> evaluation, while not excluding undocumented earlier access.**

Current manuscript and release-level prose must not claim more than this evidence supports. The
locked protocol's *requirement* that external outcomes be held out until final evaluation is a
separate statement and is not weakened; the two are different in kind and must stay distinct.

Canonical historical records keep their original wording. They are contemporaneous artifacts and
are not rewritten to match a later evidence bound.

## 2. The five wording items

### W1 — Appendix E.1 subsection title

The subsection body was corrected under A14 to use the documented-access bound, but the title
retained the stronger historical claim "executed before unblinding". Title and body must agree.
The title moves to documented-access wording. The E.2 title, "recorded and executed after
unblinding", is **not** part of this defect: the "after" direction asserts nothing beyond what the
record supports, and it is left unchanged.

### W2 — Main chronology paragraph, internal contradiction

The paragraph states the evidence bound correctly, then later converts it into an absolute
historical claim ("the external outcomes were read only afterwards"). Only that clause is
replaced, with a formulation scoped to the documented sequence. The adjacent sentence "Every prior
session record states that the external outcome had not been read" is correctly scoped to the
contents of the surviving records and is retained verbatim.

### W3 — Release README, A3 timing

The derived README says A3 was fixed "before any external outcome was read", which exceeds the
surviving evidence bound. It moves to the documented-access formulation. The canonical A3 file is
not modified.

### W4 — Release README, A13 discharge claim

The README describes A13 as achieving "Discharge of the A1 and A2 secondary reporting
obligations". A14 established that A13 discharged A2 but only partially discharged A1, because two
persisted `x_release` arms remained omitted. The heading moves to "Initial discharge", with one
clarifying sentence recording that A14 later completed A1's "in full" requirement by restoring the
persisted `C2` and `C4` `x_release` rows. This does not imply A13 was invalid, and canonical A13 is
not rewritten.

### W5 — Sampling-depth interpretation wording

The sentence "overlaps with sampling depth rather than measuring response strength directly"
asserts more than the evidence supports: strong overlap with sampling depth is established, but
absence of any direct response-strength information is not. The clause is bounded to the overlap
claim on this benchmark. The paragraph that immediately follows already supplies the non-causal
limitation, so no additional explanation is added. No adjacent numerical value changes.

figures; D-MECH and its upper-grid-boundary caveat; D-TARGET-VAR and its caveats; H-FM4's
train/validation scope; Table 7's values; and all five figure files, which are not regenerated.
The C2 sign-stability error is not reintroduced.

No style sweep is authorized. "resolved", "resolvedly" and "pre-registered" are left as they
stand, as are the title, abstract, figure and panel titles, and section structure.
