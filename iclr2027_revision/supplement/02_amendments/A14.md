## 0. What A14 is, and what it is not

A14 records defects found by independent audit of the A13/v2 release and authorizes their repair.
It completes reporting obligations that A13 began discharging but did not fully discharge.

**A14 is not** a prospective scientific lock, not a new pre-registration, not a new analysis
decision, and not authorization for further held-out evaluation.

**A14 changes no** hypothesis, endpoint, split, control, seed, feature construction, model,
estimator choice, primary head, metric, held-out access rule, scientific decision rule, or frozen
primary numerical result. It computes nothing; every figure it cites is transcribed from an
already-persisted artifact and verified against it.

## 1. A1's `x_release` obligation was only partly discharged

A1 requires (`experiment_specs/lock_amendment_A1.md:88-91`):

> "- **SECONDARY: `x_release`** — as submitted, unchanged. Reported alongside, in full, in
> the appendix.

> Both are reported for every external arm they enter. **Neither is dropped.**"

The A13/v2 release reported one `x_release` arm, `zm_xrelease`, and omitted two further persisted
`x_release` arms. A13 therefore began the discharge but did not satisfy the "in full / every
external arm" clause.

All three are reported in the corrected appendix table. Each row must make its **feature basis**

## 2. MAX-LINEAR was mis-described as count-adjusted

A4 §3.1 (`experiment_specs/lock_amendment_A4.md:53-60`) defines it:

> "`sklearn.linear_model.LinearRegression(fit_intercept=True)`, defaults otherwise, on the
> **single** feature `max|x|` — the same `‖x‖∞` component of the existing `m̃` block, z-scored
> with the frozen VCC-train scaler (§6B). Fit **once** on VCC train, applied **zero-shot** to RPE1
> and K562-essential with no target-side calibration. No tuning, no selection, no refit."

MAX-LINEAR is therefore a one-feature zero-shot linear predictor. It is **not count-adjusted**.

A distinct quantity — the count-adjusted **partial Spearman** of `max|x|` with `y` given
`log1p(n_cells)` — is reported in the main text at approximately +0.239 (RPE1) and +0.355
(K562-essential), deliberately without intervals because no interval procedure was pre-specified
or persisted for that diagnostic. MAX-LINEAR's persisted points are +0.2316 and +0.3538. The two
are different estimands and must be distinguished wherever either appears.

**MAX-LINEAR intervals are independently persisted** — `ci_lo` and `ci_hi` are present for both
screens in `phase4_secondary.json` (+0.1854/+0.2781 on RPE1; +0.3101/+0.3953 on K562-essential).
They are legitimate MAX-LINEAR intervals and are retained. They must not be removed merely because
the separate partial-Spearman diagnostic carries none, and the reporting must say explicitly which
quantity they belong to.

## 3. MAG+COUNT's mandatory disclosure was omitted

A4 §3.3 (`lock_amendment_A4.md:75-82`) records the limitation in advance and requires it be
published with the number:

> "VCC's cell counts run 10–121. RPE1's run 25–3,451 and K562's 27–1,996. The count feature is
> therefore **far outside its training range on both external screens**. A linear head extrapolates
> rather than saturating, so this does not fail the way a tree would — but the external MAG+COUNT
> number is partly an extrapolation of a coefficient fitted on a narrow range. **State this
> alongside the number.** It is recorded now so it is not discovered as a convenient explanation
> afterwards."

The A13/v2 release reported MAG+COUNT (RPE1 −0.0270 [−0.0717, +0.0173]; K562-essential +0.0327
[−0.0132, +0.0774]) without that disclosure. It is now stated alongside the result. Consistent with
A4's own framing, the disclosure is a pre-specified limitation, **not** a claim that extrapolation
caused the observed outcome.

## 4. The secondary section mis-counted its own contents

The A13/v2 prose opened "Two secondary analyses were fixed before external evaluation", while the
table carried four rows and the A1 repair adds two more. The correct framing names the families:

- **A1** — `x_release` feature-basis comparisons (three arms);
- **A2** — the ordinary linear head on `[z; m̃]`;
- **A4** — MAX-LINEAR and MAG+COUNT.

The two obligations *omitted from the previous manuscript version* were A1's and A2's; the A4
secondaries were already reported. None of the six is promoted, and none replaces the frozen
primary.
